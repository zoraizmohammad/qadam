# Roadmap — V1 / V2 / V3

De-risked in the right order: the cheapest, most decision-relevant tests come
first. A bad result on V1's fidelity study kills the concept before a dollar of
hardware time is spent.

## V1 — Prove the geometry
**Phase: bench & simulation · horizon months 0–9 · lab only, no patients**

Establish that the stave-and-membrane concept can represent a clinically
acceptable surface, and build the first dumb former + check-socket loop on the bench.

- **Software fidelity study** — error heat-maps over real rectified scans. Sweep
  stave count, placement, control points. Go/no-go gate for the whole concept.
- **Clinician-defined accuracy target** — a CPO advisor translates the residual-error
  maps into an acceptable tolerance and red-flag criteria.
- **Membrane coupon tests** — bending-skin vs. elastomer under forming pressure and
  heat. Decide the skin before the frame.
- **First manual former + PCL loop** — bench prototype: dial settings by hand, form
  a check socket, measure surface error against the digital target.

## V2 — Prove it in the field
**Phase: supervised pilot · horizon months 9–24 · CPO-supervised fittings**

Move from bench to a small, clinically supervised pilot. Validate the safety loop
on real limbs with a prosthetist present and accountable.

- **Pressure-sensing fit verification** — instrument the check-socket loop so fit
  quality is measured, not judged by eye.
- **Refined, non-uniform stave topology** — hardware reflects the placement study.
- **Calibration & maintenance protocol** — re-zero procedure, reference master, and
  a non-expert-tested setting workflow with validated timing.
- **Supervised patient fittings** — first human sockets, each signed off by a CPO
  against the V1 red-flag criteria.

## V3 — Scale & open it up
**Phase: scale & release · horizon year 2+ · deployment & open release**

Turn a validated system into a deployable, teachable, fully open platform — and
broaden from the socket toward a wider library of accessible medical hardware.

- **Full open-hardware release** — CAD, electronics-free build files, BOMs, and the
  fitting software published under open licenses, manufacturable locally.
- **Train-the-technician program** — curriculum and certification so partner orgs
  can run fittings independently and safely.
- **Regulatory pathway** — documentation and evidence package for approval in
  target deployment regions.
- **Platform expansion** — apply the durables/consumables + phone-as-brain pattern
  to orthotics and other accessible medical devices.
